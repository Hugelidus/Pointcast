Privacy

Private by default

Made for your own app on your own machine. No account, no server, no API key.

Pointcast

Runs on local dev hosts

localhost, 127.0.0.1, *.localhost, *.test out of the box. Off everywhere else.

Other sites, one host at a time

Enable a staging or preview site from the popup. Pointcast never asks for access to all sites.

Audio transcribed on your device

Whisper runs inside the extension, in your browser, Fast or Accurate. Your voice stays on your machine.

Sensitive fields never captured

Passwords, one-time codes, card fields and anything marked data-sensitive. Emails and phone numbers are redacted on enabled sites.
