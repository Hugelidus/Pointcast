Pointcast

Pointcast needs your microphone

Pointcast records what you say while you point at elements of your app, so your coding agent can tell what "this" and "here" mean. The speech is transcribed on this computer; the extension never uploads it. The recording goes to your agent's Pointcast MCP server when one is running, or else to your Downloads folder.

Chrome lets an extension ask for the microphone only from a page like this one. You allow it once; after that, Record works straight from the toolbar.

  1. Click the microphone icon at the right of the address bar, or the icon at its left for this page's settings.
  2. Set Microphone to Allow.
  3. Come back to this tab and press Check again.
